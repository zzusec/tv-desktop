Code of conduct

This project exists because a few thousand people helped each other fix their TVs in the replies to a tweet. Keep it like that.

Expected

  • Be kind. Many contributors are doing this on a parent's or grandparent's TV, in a second language, on their first pull request.
  • Assume good faith. A list that breaks something was tested on different firmware, not written to hurt anyone.
  • Say what you tested on. Specifics ("Android TV 11, build RTT1, Hisense A4") help; verdicts ("doesn't work") do not.
  • Respect the maintainers' decisions on scope. The launcher stays small and offline; if your idea does not fit, a fork with a SHOWCASE row is a good outcome, not a rejection of you.

Not accepted

  • Insults, harassment, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, Discussions or the showcase.
  • Publishing other people's private information: device identifiers, Wi-Fi names, IP addresses, ADB keys or anything that could identify a TV or a home.
  • Lists or instructions that uninstall, root, or intentionally damage a TV, or that target a specific person's device.
  • Spam, affiliate links, or passing off someone else's work as your own.

Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and block accounts that break these rules, with a short explanation. Report public concerns to the TV Desktop maintainers. For security-sensitive reports, use private vulnerability reporting. Do not include secrets or device identifiers in a public issue.

This is a short, plain-language code adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
